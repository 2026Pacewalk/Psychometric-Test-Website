**Quick answer:** TestPsychometric offers scientific psychometric testing and career counselling across all of Punjab — online, bilingual (English & Punjabi), and built for Punjab's students and families. Operated by AMG Educational Charitable Society, a registered non-profit based in Kotkapura, Faridkot.

Punjab's students are among the most ambitious in India — but career guidance here has long lagged behind aspiration. Too many talented young people choose streams and careers based on marks, pressure, or migration trends rather than their genuine strengths.

[TestPsychometric.com](/) exists to change that — a Punjab-born, Punjab-focused platform bringing scientific [psychometric testing](/blog/what-is-a-psychometric-test) and career guidance to the whole state.
